01 / XUX at first sight

Usable where humans barely can.

XUX makes complex products understandable, usable and safe for the people who operate them, in space and other extreme environments. Three focus areas:

01

HMI design

The screens, controls and tools operators actually touch.

  • Operator & ground-control UI
  • Mission tools
  • Handheld hardware
02

Human–machine interaction

How people and machines share decisions when workload spikes.

  • Alarm & layout logic
  • Navigation under stress
  • Control review
03

Extreme-environment usability

Testing what still works when the body is pushed to its limit.

  • Gloved input
  • Vibration & CO2 load
  • Time pressure
Built on NASA-STD-3001ECSS-E-ST-10-11CNumbers, not adjectives
